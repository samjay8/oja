# User interview guide

Goal: confirm or kill the two key assumptions:
1. Sellers get a meaningful share of sales from outside their city or country.
2. Sales are lost at the reply, payment or trust step.

The Google Form version of this guide is [interview-form.md](interview-form.md). Use this guide for live follow-up calls.

Ask about **past behaviour, not opinions**. Don't pitch Oja until the end, if at all. Ask permission before quoting or recording; the hackathon video needs written consent (rules: publicity, privacy and IP).

## Sellers (target 6–8: fashion, aso-ebi, tailors, crafts; in Nigeria and abroad)

1. Walk me through your last 3 sales: where did the customer find you, and how did they pay?
2. Roughly what share of last month's buyers were outside your city? Outside your country?
3. When did you last lose a sale? What happened?
4. How do you take payment from someone abroad today? What does it cost you?
5. Have you ever had a payment reversed or disputed, or money held? What happened?
6. How long do customers wait for a reply at night or weekends?
7. When someone asks "last price?", how do you decide?
8. What tools do you pay for monthly now, and how much? (Bumpa, Meta subscriptions, others)
9. Have you tried Meta's AI agent on WhatsApp or Instagram? What did it fail to do?
10. Which channels bring your sales: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, website, walk-in? Rough split?

## Buyers (diaspora and Nigeria-based)

1. Last time you bought from a seller you found on social media: how did you pay, and how did it feel?
2. Have you ever not bought because of how the seller wanted to be paid?
3. Have you been scammed, or had a near miss, buying through chat?
4. Would you pay a small fee for buyer protection? Have you before?
5. Do you prefer to chat, call, or use a website when buying from a small seller? Why?

## What to record per interview

Role · country/city · channels · cross-border share · payment methods · last lost sale (verbatim) · dispute or hold story · tools and spend · consent to quote (Y/N).

## Decision rule

- **Proceed** if several sellers describe losing sales at the payment or trust step, **and** cross-border or remote buyers are a real share of sales.
- **Rethink** if most say cross-border is rare *and* payment isn't where they lose sales.
